## New failure cases and limits on reuse

Two independently reproduced checker limitations prevent blanket source reuse:

1. The Python pre-CNF estimate is not a strict upper bound. A small symbolic
   fixture estimates8,872 flat integers but emits14,253 with990 variables.
   In particular, an n-input XOR chain emits16(n−1) integers, including clause
   terminators, whereas the estimate charges8n+8 for that node. Final ctypes/
   solver limits still reject oversized payloads, but only after Python CNF
   construction. A successor must correct the preallocation calculation before
   extending its resource contract or raising caps.

   a malformed trailing clause. The independent input `[1,0,-1,0,2]` with one
   declared variable returns−1 despite its invalid trailing literal. Thus the

   not refute a valid contradictory CNF or any saved proof here: all nine saved

   nonempty clauses and bounded clause lengths.
