## Independent proof and supplied-input obligations

Count the unoptimized time-ordered word A once, then O,A-inverse,S0,A repeated j
times. There are `1+2j` occurrences charged as A and j each of O and S0.
The inverse preserves the supplied logical T/T-dagger charge. Therefore
`C=(1+2j)A+j(O+S0)`. This is a deterministic concatenation charge, not an
optimized lower bound, T-depth, workspace, physical resource estimate or emitted
circuit measurement. R1 explicitly requires A_H=0; Clifford operations are unpriced.
Fractional charges are exact analytical inputs, not fractional emitted gates.

Each row's j, domain and O/S0 charges are supplied independently. There is no
same-j, domain-size, oracle-cost, solution-count or equal-success assumption.
Domain and event text are provenance assertions, not certificates of clean
oracle semantics, ordered mapping, matched reflection, restoration or valid-code
support. R3 removal of domain checks still needs caller-proved support. Approximate
preparation may invalidate a presumed event/support equivalence; this module does
not determine its probability or repair that premise.

Write `D=1+2j_V`, `E=j_V(O_V+S0_V)`, and use positive supplied p_H,p_V.
Equality of costs per common event is exactly

```
p_H (D A_V + E) = p_V C_H,
B = (p_V C_H - p_H E)/(p_H D),
C_V/p_V - C_H/p_H = (D/p_V)(A_V-B).
```

Here D>=1 and D/p_V>0. Hence B>0 permits improvement/tie/loss for nonnegative
A_V below/equal/above B; B=0 permits only A_V=0 to tie, every positive A_V loses;
B<0 permits no nonnegative tie or improvement. At j_V=0, D=1, E=0, so
B=(p_V/p_H)C_H even when unused O_V/S0_V are unknown. j_H=0 makes C_H=0.
These are algebraic statements at fixed supplied probabilities and other costs.
They do not assert that changing an actual preparation's cost keeps its probability
fixed, or that a physical implementation attaining B exists.

For equal positive probabilities, B reduces to the unchanged admitted raw-charge
threshold and the signed raw difference is scaled by 1/p. Unequal probabilities
can reverse a raw conclusion. Independently chosen hypothetical counterexample:
C_H=10,p_H=1/2 and j_V=0,A_V=2,p_V=1/20. Raw charge calls V cheaper (2<10),
but costs per event are 40 versus 20; B=1 and V loses by 20. This is a counterexample
to raw-threshold substitution, not an achieved graph result or candidate defect.

Unknown None is not zero. Independent literal-word accounting checked each known
