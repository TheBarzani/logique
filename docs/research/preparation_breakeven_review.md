## Independent proof assessment

### Deterministic concatenation and threshold

The time-ordered word is A once, followed by j copies of O,A-inverse,S0,A.
Assign the inverse the same supplied T/T-dagger charge as A. Direct counting
gives `C=(1+2j)A+j(O+S0)`. This is the charge of that unoptimized concatenation;
cancellation, scheduling and different circuit constructions are not inferred.
Fractional supplied charges are exact analytical quantities, not fractional
emitted gates. A deterministic static row is a caller assertion; putting a
numeric expectation into a StaticCosts field would violate that contract.

R1 explicitly requires A_H=0 logical T while Clifford operations stay unpriced.
R2/R3 carry independent domain text, iteration counts and O/S0 values. Domain
text is provenance, not a proof of equivalent predicates, support or outcomes.
In particular, R3's removed domain checks require valid support; this module
cannot certify an approximated preparation's support or oracle cleanup.

Let `D=1+2j_V>0` and `B=(C_H-j_V(O_V+S0_V))/D`. Then
`C_V-C_H=D(A_V-B)` exactly, with no same-j or same-oracle assumption.
Consequently:

- B>0: nonnegative A_V below/equal/above B improves/ties/loses in raw charge.
- B=0: A_V=0 ties; every positive A_V loses; strict improvement is impossible.
- B<0: every nonnegative A_V loses and no feasible tie exists.
- j_V=0: D=1 and B=C_H; unused O_V/S0_V do not affect the threshold.
  j_H=0 makes C_H=0 even if its unused external costs are unknown.

The implementation uses exact Fraction arithmetic and strict comparisons, so
there is no floating tolerance that collapses ties or near-boundary decisions.
Independent probes included threshold neighbours separated by 2^-200.

None is an explicit unknown, not zero. Each reached unknown retains a component
label and multiplicity; only zero-weight terms disappear. Even unused invalid
inputs are rejected at construction. Unknown A_V can coexist with a known B;
unknown reached H or valid external costs prevent a numeric threshold.
Identical unknown labels across rows are not assumed equal or cancelled.
Returning unknown when a one-sided inequality could be inferred is conservative,
not a false advantage claim. The threshold is for raw charge, not C/p.

### Success normalization and renewal

Cost-only comparison needs no inferred solution count or success probability.
C/p is exposed only with exact explicit probabilities for a caller-declared
comparable verified event, semantics text, and explicit renewal assumptions.
No M=1, domain size, solution fraction, optimal j or first-peak sweep is inferred.
The independently checked hypothetical rank reversal demonstrates that lower
raw charge need not imply lower charge per successful solution.

For identically distributed fresh independent attempts, let C be the full
unconditional attempt charge, including failures, and p the probability of
the named successful event. Conditioning on the first attempt gives
`R=E[C]+(1-p)R`, hence `R=E[C]/p` for p>0. Cost and event may be correlated
within one attempt: only future attempts must be fresh and independent with
the same distribution. The API's fixed supplied inputs are understood as
stationary across attempts. Aborted or unsuccessful completed attempts incur
their costs; measurement/reset/feed-forward and external verification remain
unpriced. These acknowledgements are assumptions, not validated infrastructure.

For RUS completion P and supplied conditional solution success q, the success
event has probability P*q by conditional probability. No independence between
completion and solution events is assumed. Renewal then uses E[C]/(P*q).
P=0 or q=0 yields an explicit undefined result, including at zero charge;
zero charge with positive success yields zero; unknown charge stays unknown.
Absent q means no solution-cost result. Direct `renewal_cost` callers must
supply unconditional E[C]; numeric typing cannot certify its provenance.
